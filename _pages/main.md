Hi there! My name is Kun. I am a PhD student in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems at Beihang University. I joined the [REMEX lab](https://remex-lab.github.io/) in 2021, where I have been advised by Professor [Zhiguo Jiang](https://remex-lab.github.io/) and Associate Professor [Yushan Zheng](https://zhengyushan.github.io/).

My research interests lie in medical image processing, histopathological image analysis, digital pathology, representation learning, self-supervised learning, deep learning, etc.
