abstract: >
  Whole slide image (WSI) classification is clinically important for computer-aided pathological diagnosis, yet manual annotation is expensive. We propose a positional encoding-guided transformer-based multiple instance learning method (PEGTB-MIL) that encodes 2D spatial position into patch features and models spatial–semantic dependencies with multi-head self-attention, with an auxiliary reconstruction task to improve consistency and generalization. Experiments on TCGA benchmarks and in-house clinical datasets demonstrate strong performance on cancer subtyping and gene mutation prediction.
